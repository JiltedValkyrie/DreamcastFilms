# Dreamcast Films

Short films rendered live by a Sega Dreamcast, by Jilted Valkyrie.

## About the author

Jilted Valkyrie is an online name. Background: theatre, customer service, bartending, software engineering, and work as a
visual effects systems technician.

- X: [@jiltedvalkyrie](https://x.com/jiltedvalkyrie)
- YouTube: [@JiltedCanon](https://www.youtube.com/@JiltedCanon)

## Films

| Film | What it is |
|---|---|
| [The Little Match Girl](LittleMatchboxGirl/) | Two minutes, narrated, after the public-domain tale by Hans Christian Andersen. A snowy night street, a bundle of matches, and what a child sees in their light. |
